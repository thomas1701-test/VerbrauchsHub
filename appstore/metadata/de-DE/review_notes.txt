The app works fully offline and needs no account.

Business model (Guideline 3.1.1): VerbrauchsHub is now free to download. On first launch a screen explains the 14-day free trial, what is locked afterwards and the one-time price. "14 Tage kostenlos testen" purchases the non-consumable "14-day Trial" (price 0), "Gleich kaufen" purchases the full version (non-consumable, 0.99 EUR). After 14 days without a purchase the app only offers buying or restoring; data stays on the device. "Kauf wiederherstellen" is available on every purchase screen and in Settings.
Customers who bought the previous paid version are recognised via AppTransaction (original app version 1–3) and keep full access without a purchase.

To explore: start the trial, then tap "Mit Beispieldaten ausprobieren" to load demo data (two buildings, seven meters, 24 months of readings). Open "Strom (Bezug)" to see the annual goal and the billing forecast.
